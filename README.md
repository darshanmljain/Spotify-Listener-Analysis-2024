# 🎵 Spotify 2024 Global Listener & Streaming Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project exploring **1.29 Trillion music streams** across **500 global market entries**. This analysis evaluates artist performance, listener reach, genre stickiness, and subscription tier dynamics (Free vs. Premium) to deliver actionable business strategies for Spotify's marketing and executive teams.

---

## 📌 Key Metrics & Findings

- **Total Global Volume**: 1.29 Trillion Streams (4.79 Million Hours Streamed)
- **Top Artist by Streams**: **BTS** (124.3 Billion Streams | 2.77 Billion Monthly Listeners)
- **Top Regional Stream Markets**: Sweden (93.4B), South Korea (87.6B), South Africa (87.4B)
- **Highest Listener Retention**: **Indie** (18.29% skip rate) and **K-pop** (19.11% skip rate)
- **Platform Volume Split**: 50.51% Premium vs. 49.49% Free

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

- **Environment**: Google Colab / Python 3.12
- **Data Wrangling**: Pandas, NumPy
- **Visualizations**: Seaborn, Matplotlib
- **Dataset**: `Spotify_2024_Global_Streaming_Data.csv` (500 records, 12 attributes)

---

## 💡 Strategic Business Recommendations

1. **Promote Low-Skip Genres**: Feature **Indie** and **K-pop** prominently on editorial playlists due to their superior listener retention.
2. **Capitalize on High-Reach Regional Markets**: Expand localized marketing partnerships in **Russia**, **Japan**, and **South Africa**.
3. **Refine Premium Subscription Messaging**: Focus conversion ads on ad-free listening and offline playback, as listening duration is identical between Free and Premium tiers.
